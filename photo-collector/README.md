# photo-collector

Gathers photos and videos of Matt's kids, downloaded from many sources, into one collection:
finds and removes duplicates, and sorts the files into buckets (by year, photos split from
videos, and similar).

Only the subfolder exists so far; the collection logic comes in later items. Photos and videos
are never committed here: the source and destination folders are passed in as settings.
